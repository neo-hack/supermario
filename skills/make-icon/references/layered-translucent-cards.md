# Layered Translucent Cards

Use this reference for a polished product icon built from a small stack of distinct translucent and opaque planes.

## Style DNA

- **Structure:** full-bleed base, one or two offset translucent middle planes, and one dominant opaque foreground plane.
- **Shape:** rounded rectangles, soft slabs, or simple closed panels with clearly separated silhouettes.
- **Edge:** crisp foreground edges; translucent layers may carry restrained rim light or refraction.
- **Depth:** overlap, offset, scale, and layer order create space before shadow does; shadows remain broad and shallow.
- **Color:** analogous gradient base, tinted transparent middle layers, high-value foreground, and one compact accent.
- **Composition:** centered stack with controlled tilt and 55–72% foreground scale; preserve safe area around all rotated corners.

## Prompt Keywords

`layered translucent cards`, `floating rounded planes`, `soft glassmorphism`, `clear layer hierarchy`, `tinted transparency`, `controlled refraction`, `subtle rim light`, `shallow inter-layer shadow`, `analogous gradient field`, `opaque focal plate`, `premium product icon`

## Adjustable Axes

- Stack: two, three, or four semantic layers.
- Alignment: centered, diagonally offset, fanned, or gently rotated.
- Material: frosted, clear tinted, milky translucent, or opaque satin.
- Depth: nearly flat, lightly floating, or moderately separated.

## Keep Out

Avoid uncontrolled transparency, too many panes, strong lens distortion, rainbow refraction, sharp glass shards, deep perspective, floating UI screenshots, text, and decorative layers without semantic purpose.

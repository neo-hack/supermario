# Hand-Drawn Ink Object

Use this reference for an app icon that translates a recognizable everyday object into a sparse, slightly imperfect black-ink drawing. It can also produce an abstract partition symbol when the user explicitly asks for abstraction. The subject is an input, not a fixed motif.

## Style DNA

- **Recognition:** the outer contour reads as the requested object before its interior is examined. Keep the few functional parts needed to distinguish it from a generic frame or blob.
- **Drawing:** one confident, heavy felt-tip or brush-pen stroke family. Edges waver gently; joins are blunt and organic. Keep thickness broadly consistent rather than making perfect vector paths or scratchy sketches.
- **Interior:** use zero to three broad curved strokes. Let them describe a real division, visible surface, or simple graphic within the object. Every stroke has a clear endpoint; avoid unexplained tangles.
- **Focal point:** at most one small solid dot, only when it helps the object or composition. It can act as a control, lens, target, or abstract accent; do not force it into every subject.
- **Color:** default to deep charcoal ink on a solid warm off-white ground. The user may change either hue while preserving strong two-color contrast.
- **Mood:** naive modernist, calm, tactile in the stroke only; no simulated material volume.

## Subject and Composition

- Draw one object per image, centered and upright on a direct `1:1` square canvas. Size its complete silhouette to roughly `68–82%` of the square, adjusting for long handles or narrow subjects so no part feels cramped.
- Use a clear frontal or simple profile view. Preserve basic geometry: round parts stay round, square parts stay square, and attached parts meet cleanly.
- Keep the solid background visible in every open area, including inside the object. Deliver an opaque canvas; transparent gaps may display as black in an app preview.
- For a requested set with unspecified subjects, choose distinct recognizable objects from different everyday categories. Vary the subject and its meaningful internal structure, not just the same abstract enclosure's curve positions.
- For an explicitly abstract request, let one rounded enclosure and a few connected partitions form the mark. Do not turn a named object into an abstract partition unless the user asks.

## Small-Size Check

At `32 × 32`, the outer silhouette must identify the object and the main attachment must remain visible. Reduce interior curves before simplifying away the identifying part. Keep ample spacing between dark strokes so open regions do not close up.

## Prompt Keywords

`recognizable everyday object`, `bold hand-drawn ink contour`, `slightly wavering felt-tip stroke`, `blunt organic joins`, `one to three broad interior curves`, `plain warm off-white ground`, `two-color high contrast`, `open negative space`, `small-size readability`

## Keep Out

Avoid thin pencil hatching, many construction lines, realistic detail, shaded fills, gradients, paper grain, faux aging, drop shadows, glow, bevels, 3D, photorealism, text, logos, decorative frames, multiple objects, and transparent backgrounds. Do not copy a source video's exact line arrangement when only its style is requested.
